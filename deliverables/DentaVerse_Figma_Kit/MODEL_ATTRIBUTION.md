## Changes in DentaVerse

The bundled GLB bytes are unchanged. Runtime transforms re-center and normalize the model, orient the crown upwards and mirror the left specimen for the right-side learning activity. The renderer adjusts lighting and material roughness. These changes are adaptations, not an endorsement by the University of Dundee.

The Dentin view uses a scaled, recolored surface as an illustrative envelope. The Pulp view is an original schematic chamber/canal illustration. Those views are not CT segmentations from the source model and are labelled illustrative in the interface.

Independent anatomy/faculty review of the mirrored model and internal illustrations remains pending. The model is for this educational interaction exercise, not diagnosis, treatment planning or measurement.
